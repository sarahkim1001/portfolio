Ouroboros: Cave of Forgotten Dreams

Ouroboros invites a gentle pause within a tunnel of grass and bubble wrap, a burrow where memory and dreams can breathe. Bluetooth headphones carry a shifting soundscape—weather, urban whispers, digital echoes, and soft twists of Debussy’s Clair de Lune—guiding the heart inward. Cloud projections drift around, stirring nostalgia for a lost innocence, a child’s wonder. This space, both tender and confined, holds us amid society’s rush, inviting mindfulness. Inspired by Pauline Oliveros, it asks: What thoughts arise? What calms the self? Ouroboros softens the grip of digital noise, transforming escapism into presence. Here, we touch forgotten dreams, find stillness in chaos, and reclaim a quiet power in a world that blurs.
